Introduction

Welcome to Bible Buddies Chat! We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we collect, use, and safeguard your information. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection and usage when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Personal Information You Provide

When you create an account with Bible Buddies, we collect:

  • Email Address: Used for account creation and authentication
  • Username: Your chosen display name within the app
  • Gender: Optional demographic information to personalize your experience
  • Google Account Information: If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email, and profile picture from Google

Chat and Usage Data

  • Chat Conversations: Your conversations with biblical characters are stored securely in our cloud database
  • Language Preferences: Your selected language for personalized experience
  • App Activity: Information about which characters you chat with and how you use the app

Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers
  • Usage Statistics: App features used, session duration, and interaction patterns
  • Technical Data: Error logs and crash reports to improve app stability

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do NOT track your precise location
  • We do NOT access your contacts, photos, or camera without permission
  • We do NOT collect payment information (donations are processed by third-party services)
  • We do NOT share your data for advertising purposes

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Create and manage your user account
  • Authenticate you securely via Google Sign-In
  • Store and sync your chat conversations across devices
  • Provide personalized content based on your preferences
  • Improve app functionality and user experience
  • Send important account and service notifications
  • Fix bugs and resolve technical issues
  • Analyze usage patterns to enhance features
  • Ensure compliance with our terms of service

Data Storage and Security

Where Your Data is Stored

  • Supabase Cloud Database: Your account information and chat history are stored securely on Supabase servers
  • Google Authentication: Authentication tokens are managed by Google's secure infrastructure
  • Data Centers: Data may be stored in data centers located in various countries

Security Measures

  • Encryption: All data is encrypted in transit using industry-standard SSL/TLS protocols
  • Secure Authentication: Google OAuth 2.0 provides secure, token-based authentication
  • Access Controls: Strict access controls limit who can access your data
  • Regular Security Audits: We regularly review and update our security practices
⚠️ Important: While we implement strong security measures, no system is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

Children's Privacy

Age Requirement: Bible Buddies Chat is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental supervision.

For Users Under 13

  • Parental Supervision Required: Children under 13 should only use this app with active parental supervision and consent
  • Parent Responsibility: Parents are responsible for monitoring their child's use of Bible Buddies Chat
  • Account Creation: Parents may create and manage accounts on behalf of their children
  • No Targeted Collection: We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental involvement

For Parents and Guardians

Bible Buddies Chat contains age-appropriate religious and educational content. However, parents should be aware:

  • The app requires account creation via Google Sign-In
  • We collect email address, username, and optional gender information
  • Chat conversations are stored to provide the service
  • All content is based on biblical text and is educational in nature
  • We do not display advertisements or third-party behavioral tracking

Parental Rights and Controls

Parents have the right to:

  • Review: Request to see what data has been collected about their child
  • Delete: Request deletion of their child's account and all associated data
  • Refuse Further Collection: Request that we stop collecting data about their child
  • Contact Us: Email futureminds.space@gmail.com for any concerns

If We Discover a Child Under 13

If we discover that a child under 13 has provided personal information without parental supervision or consent, we will:

  1. Attempt to notify the parent at the registered email address
  2. Delete the account and all associated data if parental consent cannot be verified
  3. Take steps to prevent re-registration

COPPA Compliance

We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) by:

  • Not targeting children under 13 as our primary audience
  • Not knowingly collecting personal information from children under 13 without parental involvement
  • Providing parents with control over their children's information
  • Not conditioning participation on providing more information than necessary
  • Maintaining reasonable security procedures to protect children's information
Notice to Parents: If you believe your child under 13 has created an account without your supervision or consent, please contact us immediately at futureminds.space@gmail.com with the subject line "Child Account - Under 13" and we will promptly delete the account.

How Parents Can Exercise These Rights

To exercise any of these rights:

  1. Email us at futureminds.space@gmail.com
  2. Use subject line: "Parental Rights Request - Bible Buddies Chat"
  3. Provide:
    • Your name and email address
    • Your child's registered email address
    • Proof of parental relationship (may be requested for verification)
    • Specific request (review, delete, or refuse further collection)
  4. We will respond within 10 business days

Your Data Rights

Access and Control

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data via email
  • Correction: Request update or correction of inaccurate information via email
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and all associated data via email
  • Export: Request download of your chat history and personal information via email
  • Opt-Out: Request to opt-out of analytics and optional data collection via email

How to Exercise Your Rights

All data rights requests must be submitted via email:

  1. Email us at futureminds.space@gmail.com
  2. Specify which right you wish to exercise (access, correction, deletion, export, opt-out)
  3. Provide verification of your identity (registered email address)
  4. We will respond within 30 days
