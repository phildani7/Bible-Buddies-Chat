🌟 What is Bible Buddies Chat?

Bible Buddies Chat is an innovative educational app that brings the Bible to life through interactive conversations. Chat with over 50 biblical characters from the Old and New Testament, ask questions, learn their stories, and explore Scripture in an engaging, conversational way. Available in 45+ languages, making biblical education accessible to everyone around the world!

p>
📢 Age Requirement: Bible Buddies Chat is designed for users aged 13 and older. Children under 13 should use the app only with active parental supervision and consent.

Bible Buddies Chat uses AI technology to create educational conversations based on biblical text and historical context.
