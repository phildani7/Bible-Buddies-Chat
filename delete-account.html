🗑️ Delete Your Bible Buddies Chat Account

We're sorry to see you go! If you'd like to delete your Bible Buddies Chat account and all associated data, please follow the instructions below.

⚠️ Important: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data, including chat history, profile information, and preferences will be permanently deleted from our servers.

What Will Be Deleted

When you request account deletion, the following data will be permanently removed:

  • Your email address and username
  • Your profile information (gender, preferences)
  • All your chat conversations with biblical characters
  • Your language preferences and settings
  • Any other personal data associated with your account

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email to our support team at:
    futureminds.space@gmail.com
  2. Use this subject line:
    "Account Deletion Request - Bible Buddies Chat"
  3. Include in your email:
    • Your registered email address (the one you use to sign in with Google)
    • Confirmation that you want to delete your account
    • Any additional feedback (optional)
  4. Wait for confirmation: We will process your request within 7 business days and send you a confirmation email once deletion is complete.
📧 Quick Email Template:

To: futureminds.space@gmail.com
Subject: Account Deletion Request - Bible Buddies Chat

Body:
"I would like to delete my Bible Buddies Chat account.
My registered email address is: [YOUR EMAIL]
I understand this action is permanent and cannot be undone."

📧 Send Deletion Request Email

Processing Time

  • Verification: We will verify your identity using your registered email address
  • Processing: Account deletion will be completed within 7 business days
  • Confirmation: You will receive an email confirming the deletion
  • Backup Removal: Data in backups will be removed within 90 days

What Happens After Deletion

  • ✅ All your personal data will be permanently deleted
  • ✅ You will no longer be able to sign in to Bible Buddies Chat
  • ✅ Your chat history will be completely erased
  • ✅ We cannot recover your account after deletion

Alternative Options

Partial Data Deletion (Keep Your Account)

Don't want to delete your entire account? You can request deletion of specific data only:

  • Delete chat history only - Keep your account and profile, remove all conversations
  • Delete profile information - Keep your account and chat history, remove personal info
  • Delete specific conversations - Remove only selected chat history

To request partial deletion, email us at futureminds.space@gmail.com with:

  • Subject line: "Partial Data Deletion Request - Bible Buddies Chat"
  • Specify exactly what you want deleted
  • Include your registered email address

Data Export

If you'd like to keep a copy of your data before deletion, you can request a data export:

  • Email us at futureminds.space@gmail.com
  • Subject line: "Data Export Request - Bible Buddies Chat"
  • We will send you a copy of your data within 30 days

Children's Privacy

Age Requirement: Bible Buddies Chat is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental supervision.

For Users Under 13

  • Parental Supervision Required: Children under 13 should only use this app with active parental supervision and consent
  • Parent Responsibility: Parents are responsible for monitoring their child's use of Bible Buddies Chat
  • Account Creation: Parents may create and manage accounts on behalf of their children
  • No Targeted Collection: We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental involvement

For Parents Requesting Child Account Deletion

If you are a parent requesting deletion of your child's account:

  • Email us at futureminds.space@gmail.com
  • Subject line: "Child Account Deletion - Bible Buddies Chat"
  • Include:
    • Your child's registered email address
    • Your relationship to the child (parent/guardian)
    • Confirmation of deletion request
  • We may request verification of parental relationship
  • Account will be deleted within 7 business days
